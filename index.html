  • A one-command release pipeline that ships an app to iPhone, Android, the web, Mac, and Windows at the same time.
  • A personal knowledge system that keeps track of my projects, classes, and goals across every session, so context is never lost between days of work.
  • A quantitative research lab with a tested toolkit that I use for stock research at AIC.

Connect Support

Connect suggests one concrete thing to go do each day: something outside, something with another person in person, or something on your own.

Need help or found a problem? Email spencer16078@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working. I read every message.

How are the suggestions chosen?

When you first open the app you answer a short survey about where you live, what you can get to, and what you enjoy. Connect uses those answers to rank its library of activities, so it will not suggest something that needs a trail or a lake if you cannot easily reach one. The suggestion stays the same for the whole day and changes the next day.

The suggestions don't fit me anymore

Go to Settings → Retake the survey. Your new answers replace the old ones, and the suggestions update right away.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row that you have marked something as done. If yesterday was your last day, the streak is still alive today. If a full day passes with nothing done, it starts over at one the next time. You can also add a short note when you mark something as done, so you can remember how it went.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Connect has no accounts and no server, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to sync. This also means your history does not move to a new phone unless your device backup carries it over. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings → Erase everything. This permanently removes your survey answers, history, notes, and streak from your phone.

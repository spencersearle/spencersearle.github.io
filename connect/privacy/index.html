Connect Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Connect does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app never sends it anywhere.

What the app stores

To work, Connect saves a small amount of information in your phone's local storage:

  • Your answers to the setup survey, and the profile built from them.
  • The activities you mark as done, and any note you add to one.
  • Your current streak.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • It does not connect to any server. The app makes no network requests.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • It does not share or sell data to third parties, because it has no data to share.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Erase everything inside the app. Deleting the app from your phone also removes all of its data.

Backups

If you use iCloud or another device backup, your phone's operating system may include Connect's local data in that backup, the same way it does for other apps. That backup is controlled by your device settings and your backup provider, not by Connect.

Children

Connect is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Connect ever begins collecting data, this page will say so before that version of the app is released.
